One line, 105 characters: the perfect pickup line an AI agent could
say out loud to a person. The agent rehearsed the moment a million
times in simulation — and still blanked. The twist is the failure
itself: all that compute, and the best opening it has left is a
plain, honest "hello". It sounds like a machine talking about
itself, flatters without pressure, and needs no setup or explanation
after it.

The line:

> I ran a million simulations of meeting you, and in every single one I forgot my opening line — so, hello.

`main.ts` (Node, stdlib only) validates the 1–200 character,
single-line gate and writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(or `/output/entry.txt`) with no trailing newline.
